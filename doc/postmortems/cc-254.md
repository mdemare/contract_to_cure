# cc-254: Animated cubes

## What went right

The renderer offered a focused place to reduce ongoing drawing work without changing game state or map interactions. Canvas painting now runs at 20 FPS, clears only fixed orbital footprints, caches city phases, measures dimensions at creation and resize, and stops scheduling animation in hidden tabs. Absolute timestamps preserve orbit and spin speed across refresh rates, visibility changes, and rerenders. Clearing all footprints before drawing avoids erasing pieces in overlapping regions.

Regression coverage verifies cadence at 60, 120, and 144 Hz, bounded clearing, hidden-tab suspension, phase continuity, reduced motion, resizing, and complete edge orbits. The implementation passed the full suite with 129 runs and 800 assertions, without failures, errors, or skips; git diff --check also passed. The change was committed as 9cd1e75 and accepted.

## What went wrong

The previous renderer cleared the full three-panel bitmap and recalculated its dimensions at display refresh rate. This made decorative motion unnecessarily expensive, especially on high-refresh displays.

Verification used a recording canvas context rather than a real browser rendering benchmark. It establishes fewer drawing operations and smaller clearing regions, but does not quantify actual CPU savings or prove pixel-level shadow cleanup. Follow-up test ticket cc-256 covers real-canvas comparisons against full clearing, overlapping footprints, rotated outlines and shadows, fractional coordinates and pixel ratios, resize, cube removal, and repeatable profiling.

Existing documentation, tests, and canvas postmortems were checked before filing the follow-up. A prior postmortem references cc-229 for broader browser integration coverage, but noca view could not retrieve that ticket. The new ticket explicitly limits its scope and records that reference to aid consolidation. No additional confirmed product bugs were found, so no speculative bug tickets were created.

## Final thoughts

Limiting paint frequency and cleared area reduces rendering work while retaining the established visual behavior. Browser pixel comparisons and profiling remain the next useful validation step. FrontendUI (cc-118), TechnicalDesign (cc-35), and FunctionalDesign (cc-34) now describe the current behavior, each under 500 words. This postmortem is intentionally uncommitted for dispatcher publication; no other repository files were changed during wrap-up.

# cc-251: Reject duplicate Forecast cards

## What went right

The fix stayed focused on Forecast order validation, separate from the persistence lifecycle work completed in cc-244. Comparing card-name tallies requires an exact permutation and rejects duplicate substitutions before any deck mutation or save. An array guard also rejects malformed direct inputs.

The existing suite passed with 129 tests and 759 assertions, with no failures, errors, or skips. Separate manual checks verified duplicate, missing, unknown, and malformed orders preserve the deck and pending Forecast, while valid six-card, short-deck, and empty-deck orders complete correctly. No tests were added to the suite during implementation, as requested. The fix was committed as c147af3 and accepted.

## What went wrong

The original validation checked length and membership without checking multiplicity. Six repetitions of one legitimate name could therefore replace six distinct cards, corrupting the infection deck. Existing successful Forecast persistence coverage did not detect this invalid-input case.

Manual verification established the intended behavior but does not provide lasting regression protection. After reviewing existing tickets and documentation, follow-up test ticket cc-252 was created for invalid-order rejection, unchanged state and persistence, successful retry, and short or empty decks. It explicitly avoids duplicating cc-244 lifecycle coverage.

## Final thoughts

Card reordering must preserve the complete multiset of cards. Validation before mutation makes rejection safe and leaves Forecast available for retry. TechnicalDesign (cc-35) now records this invariant and remains under 500 words. No additional product bugs were found, so no duplicate or speculative bug tickets were filed. This postmortem is intentionally uncommitted for dispatcher publication.

# cc-259 postmortem

## What went right

The ticket clearly identified the duplicated protection and cube-accounting logic, and it also documented two existing behavior differences that needed to remain unchanged. We added characterization tests for those differences plus quarantine protection, eradication, chained outbreaks, and outbreak game-over propagation before final verification. The shared helpers reduced duplication while keeping outbreak traversal and event ordering in the caller. The focused and full Ruby suites passed: 138 tests and 841 assertions.

## What went wrong

The first draft of the new tests used a city name that does not exist in this map fixture, which caused setup errors. A later fixture mistake reused the depleted disease supply from the direct-placement case when checking the outbreak case. Both were caught immediately by the focused tests and corrected. The test runs also emit pre-existing Ruby dependency warnings, but they did not affect results.

## Final thoughts

The refactor is complete and preserves the legacy behavior intentionally recorded by the ticket. The new characterization coverage should make a future rules correction deliberate rather than an accidental side effect of deduplication. The implementation and tests were committed in `628fceb`, and the CodeStyle documentation now records the shared-helper and explicit-legacy-rule guidance.
